# Critic

Generate a packet only from a current successful evaluation. Give only the packet to the reviewer, avoiding protected full records. The reviewer returns the response_template with identity, substantive summary, fresh_context boolean, all warnings_reviewed, and finding dispositions. Findings require unique IDs, kind, severity, message, status and response. Open findings block. Technical defects cannot be accepted-risk or rejected. Preserve previous findings across revisions. Quick/standard/thorough budgets are 1/2/3 revision rounds, one closing round and at most one verification. Closing/verification must account for previous findings and may not introduce new subjective must-fix findings. Budget exhaustion yields diagnostics, never a bypass. If no fresh context exists, mark false in the audit.
