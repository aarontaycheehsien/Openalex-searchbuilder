# Anti Patterns

Do not AND every detail of the question. Do not copy PubMed tags or pretend topics are MeSH. Do not require abstracts, English, open access, journal articles or recent dates without protocol reasons. Do not translate publication-date limits into historical indexing claims. Do not mine validation/benchmark records. Never report fixture counts as real evidence, infer misses from local substring matching, or call a relevance sample a precision estimate. Do not silently switch exact/stemmed matching or corpus to satisfy a query error.
